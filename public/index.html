Status guide
  • Proven: a referenced allowlisted check ran and passed in the claim's evidence class.
  • Conditional: a result was declared but not runner-verified, or evidence came from a different class.
  • Simulated: the evidence is a synthetic fixture, not live behavior.
  • Unverified: evidence is missing or a required check failed.
